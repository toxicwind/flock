## SKINWALKER RANCH FEDERAL INVESTMENT

The ranch was the primary field site for AAWSAP (2008–2012). BAASS personnel documented:
- Unidentified aerial phenomena
- Biological effects on personnel
- Anomalous electromagnetic signatures
- Cattle mutilations
- Portal-like phenomena

Total federal expenditure on ranch investigation: estimated **$22 million** via DIA black budget.

## CURRENT STATUS

As of 2026, the property is privately owned by **Brandon Fugal**. Federal interest continues through:
- AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office)
- Congressional UAP hearings (2022–present)
- Radiance Technologies (Stratton + Taylor)
- History Channel production (unofficial federal liaison through retired personnel)
